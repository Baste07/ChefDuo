OUR JOURNEY

ChefDuo started with a simple idea: bold, spicy flavors shouldn't have to choose between comfort food and craft. What began as two friends experimenting with sauces and seasonings in a small kitchen in Bagumbayan grew into a menu built around heat, crunch, and generous portions.

Every dish, from our Spicy Herb Chicken to the Cheesy Breaded Porkchop, is made to order with fresh ingredients and a house blend of spices we've refined over the years. We're not a fine-dining spot, and we're proud of that. We're the place you come back to when you want food that actually tastes like someone cared about it.
